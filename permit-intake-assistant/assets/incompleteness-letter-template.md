# Incompleteness letter — template

Fill the bracketed fields. Keep the tone factual and neutral: per BDC 50.25.3, incompleteness is based
solely on missing required information or unaddressed criteria — never on quality or the merits. Do not
state or imply a likely decision. List only items the applicant must still provide.

**Hyperlink every BDC citation and every defined term** to encodePlus, using
`https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/beaverton-or/doc-viewer.aspx#secid-<ID>` with the IDs in
`references/secid-sections.tsv` and `references/secid-definitions.tsv` (see SKILL.md → Hyperlinking).

---

City of Beaverton — Community Development Department
Planning Division

[DATE]

[APPLICANT NAME]
[APPLICANT ADDRESS]

RE: Application [CASE NUMBER] — [APPLICATION TYPE] at [SITE ADDRESS] ([ZONE])

Dear [APPLICANT NAME]:

The City has reviewed the above application for completeness under Beaverton Development Code (BDC)
Section 50.25. The application is **incomplete**. Before the City can begin review on the merits, please
provide the following:

**Missing or deficient submittal items**

1. [Item] — [what specifically is missing or deficient]. Required by [BDC citation, e.g. 50.25.1.B / 40.60.15.1.D / 60.40.35.1.A].
2. [Item] — [detail]. Required by [citation].
3. [...]

**Applicable standards the submittal must address**

This [APPLICATION TYPE] is reviewed against the following standards. Your written statement and exhibits
should demonstrate compliance with each:

- [BDC citation] — [short description of the standard]
- [BDC citation] — [short description]
- [...]

**Next steps and timeline**

Per BDC 50.25, you may respond by submitting (a) all of the missing information; (b) some of it with written
notice that no other information will be provided; or (c) written notice that none will be provided. The
application will be deemed complete upon one of these. An application on file more than 180 days without a
response becomes void (BDC 50.25.9).

If you have questions, please contact me.

Sincerely,

[PLANNER NAME]
[TITLE], City of Beaverton Planning Division
[PHONE] · [EMAIL]

---

## Notes for the planner (do not include in the letter)
- If everything required is present, do not produce a deficiency letter — instead note the application
  appears complete and is ready for review on the merits, and list the standards it will be reviewed against.
- Cite the exact subsection for each item so the applicant can find it. Prefer the most specific citation.
- Keep "applicable standards" tied to the approval criteria for the specific application type and zone.

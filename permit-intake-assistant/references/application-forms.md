# Official application forms — submittal checklists & approval criteria

These checklists are transcribed from the City of Beaverton's own application forms (Planning Division,
linked from https://beavertonoregon.gov/777/Applications-Fees-Brochures). The city's form IS the submittal
checklist, so build the planner's checklist from it, then confirm against BDC 50.25.1 and the approval
criteria. **Fees appear on every form, but this skill assumes they are collected before review — don't list fees as missing.**
Form links can change; if one 404s, return to the Applications/Fees/Brochures page for the current asset.

---

## Wall / Freestanding Sign Permit
Form: "Wall / Freestanding Sign Permit Application" · Approval criteria: BDC 40.60.15.1.C · Standards: 60.40

**Header info the form collects (verify present):** property owner/manager + signature; business owner/tenant;
sign company (a sign company may sign with the owner/tenant's consent); site address; Washington County tax
lot #; building/shopping-center name; **number of proposed wall/blade signs and freestanding signs; zone;
"sign inventory on file?" (Y/N)**.

**Submittal items (two copies of each; application itself one copy):**
1. **Sign Inventory** — complete inventory of all existing wall/blade/awning signs on the building, unless one
   is already on file with the City (Wall Sign Inventory worksheet or the Excel template). Existing signs count
   toward the allowance (60.40.30.1).
2. **Close-up sign rendering** — length and height of the sign and of individual letters; whether illuminated
   and the method of illumination; for an EMC, the ratio of EMC to total sign area (%) and brightness/intensity/
   duration.
3. **Site plan** (a clear aerial photo is acceptable) — entire lot showing sign location relative to adjacent
   buildings, parking spaces, driveways, landscaped areas, property lines, rights-of-way; locations of all
   existing freestanding/projecting signs; **Sight Clearance Areas (freestanding signs only)**.
4. **Wall elevations (wall signs only)** — locations of the proposed signs showing dimensioned height on the wall.
5. **Primary Building Wall calculation** — wall face being used (N/S/E/W), wall height (max 25 ft), wall length,
   wall area, and maximum allowed sign area (= wall area × 20%). Max any one wall sign = 120 sq ft.

For a "3 new wall signs" proposal, the planner is verifying items 1–5 with the per-sign size/area filled in for
each of the three signs and the cumulative total within the 20% Primary Building Wall allowance.

## Design Review Compliance Letter (DRCL) — commercial/non-residential Type 1
Form: "Design Review Compliance Letter Application" (Rev 01/25) · Approval criteria: BDC 40.20.15.1.C

**Project info the form collects:** existing site size; existing building sq ft; **sq ft of proposed building
addition**; building permit number; existing/proposed parking spaces; existing/proposed landscaped area; zoning
district; map & tax lot. (Use these to test the DRCL threshold: addition ≤25% of existing GFA and ≤2,500 sq ft
in residential/commercial/MU; ≤15% and <30,000 sq ft industrial — above that it's DR Two/Three, not Type 1.)

**Worksheet flags (each "yes" pulls in standards):** exterior modifications (60.05.15); new rooftop equipment
(screening, 60.05.15.5); abuts a Major Pedestrian Route (the form cites 60.05.55, which is repealed; don't cite it); loading/solid-waste/mechanical
(60.05.20.2); parking/walkway changes (60.05.20.3–9); landscape changes (60.05.25 — needs landscape plan);
retaining wall/fence (60.05.25.8 retaining walls, 60.05.25.9 fences and walls; the form says 9–10); grading (grading plan; 60.15.10 if in/abutting residential); lighting
(60.05.30 + Table 60.05-1); loading (60.25); parking (60.30); **tree removal — contact staff; if Landscape
Trees, address 60.60.25.9 in the written statement**.

**Submittal checklist:**
1. Application form + Design Review Project Information + checklist, original signature.
2. Written statement — detailed project description (site, structure, landscaping, parking, land use; location,
   materials, sizes, colors, sq ft).
3. Site plan (entire property) labeling abutting streets, property lines, setbacks, structures, parking,
   driveways, landscaped areas, proposed sq ft, easements/utilities within 25 ft of modifications, and existing/
   approved vehicular/pedestrian/bicycle connections; parking-count reconciliation if parking changes.
4. Architectural elevations — character, dimensions, materials, colors, textures of buildings/structures.
5. Approved Land Division Housing Plan (or concurrent amendment) — only if changes decrease dwelling units or
   affect visitability.
6. Clean Water Services documentation — if the proposal includes a building addition/new construction or grading/
   paving (may be satisfied by a City pre-screen at a pre-app conference).

## Single-Detached & Middle Housing Design Review One (SDMHDR1) — residential Type 1
Form: "Single-Detached and Middle Housing Design Review Application" (Rev 01/25) · Criteria: BDC 40.21.15.1.C

The form covers Types One/Two/Three — confirm **Type 1** (proposal meets all applicable standards; choosing to
address a Type 2/3 guideline instead of a standard bumps it to that type, outside this skill's scope).

**Worksheet flags → standards (Chapter 60.05.60 tables by housing type):** new construction in RMA/RMB/RMC/
CM-RM (20.05.15, 20.22.15/.35, 20.25.05, 20.25.10, 20.30.10; 60.05.60 tables; 60.30; 60.55.15/.25);
window/door add/relocate (60.05.60.2 S1–S3, etc.); **floor-area additions** (20.05.15 or 20.22.15 setbacks/FAR/
height; garage rules 60.30.10/.15, 60.50.05); Landscape Tree removal (60.60.25.9 + 60.05.60 tree standards);
landscaping; common courtyards/community buildings (cottage clusters); grading within 25 ft of a property line
(60.15.10); garage/parking/circulation changes; parking-lot lighting (>1,400 sq ft → Table 60.05-1);
CM-RM small-scale commercial (20.22.35).

**Submittal checklist (include only the plans applicable to the proposal — e.g. a window-only change needs no
tree info):** application form (orig. signature); checklist; **written statement addressing each Chapter 20 and
Chapter 60 provision and each 40.21.15.1.C approval criterion individually** ("not applicable"/boilerplate is
rejected); existing conditions plan; dimensioned site plan; grading plan; utility plan; landscape plan; lighting
plan; architectural elevations; Land Division Housing Plan (if units decrease/visitability); service-provider
docs (CWS/TVF&R/BSD). Pre-app notes and Neighborhood Review Meeting materials are **Type 3 only** (encouraged
for Type 2) — not required for Type 1.

## Legal Lot Determination (LLD) — Type 1
Form: "Property Line Adjustment, Replat for Lot Consolidation, and Legal Lot Determination Application"
(Rev 08/2016) · LLD approval criteria: BDC 40.47.15.1.C

**Property info:** existing use; site address; assessor's map & tax lot; lot size; zoning district.

**Submittal checklist:**
1. Application form (original signature).
2. Completed checklist.
3. Written statement (3 copies) — addresses applicable Chapter 20 provisions (min land area, lot dimensions,
   yard setbacks) and provides individual findings for each 40.47 approval criterion.
4. Replat/LLD plan (3 copies, to scale) — existing and proposed property lines/sizes/dimensions; existing public
   & private easements and utilities on/abutting the property; 100-year floodplain location if applicable.
5. **Deed History and Title Report** — may be requested if staff cannot determine the unit is a "Lot of Record"
   (Chapter 90 definition).
6. Clean Water Services documentation (50.25.1.F).

LLD approval criteria (40.47.15.1.C): threshold met; fees submitted; the unit of land conforms to Chapter 20
area/dimensional standards (with the pre-2007 lawful-creation exception and the ORS 215.755 dwelling exception);
submittal complete per 50.25.1; related approvals sequenced.

## Other application types
For Adjustment, Conditional Use, Downtown DR/DRCL, Director's Interpretation, Home Occupation, Land Division,
Loading/Parking Determination, Temporary Use, Tree Plan, Variance, etc., the same page hosts a form. Fetch the
form, read its **submittal checklist** and **approval-criteria** pages, and confirm the procedure type — only
Type 1 is in scope.

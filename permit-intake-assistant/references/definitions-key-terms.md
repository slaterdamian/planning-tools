# Chapter 90 — full text of decision-critical definitions

These are the BDC Chapter 90 definitions most often needed to make an intake determination. Full text is
captured here so the skill can reason offline. For any other defined term, use `secid-definitions.tsv` to
build the encodePlus link and read it there. Each term below also links to its secid.

> Link format: `https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/beaverton-or/doc-viewer.aspx#secid-<ID>`

## Signs

**[Sign](https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/beaverton-or/doc-viewer.aspx#secid-86) (secid 86).** Any lettered or pictorial device designed to inform or attract attention.

**[Wall Sign](https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/beaverton-or/doc-viewer.aspx#secid-502) (secid 502).** A sign attached to, erected against or painted on an exterior wall of a building.

**[Freestanding Sign](https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/beaverton-or/doc-viewer.aspx#secid-486) (secid 486).** A sign supported by one or more uprights, poles or braces placed in or upon the ground, or a sign supported by any structure primarily for the display and support of the sign.

**[Projecting Sign](https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/beaverton-or/doc-viewer.aspx#secid-496) (secid 496).** A sign larger in face size than a blade sign, that is attached to a building and typically placed perpendicular to the primary building wall.

**[Blade Sign](https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/beaverton-or/doc-viewer.aspx#secid-1077) (secid 1077).** A sign which hangs from the face of a building that is supported by an extended arm from the building wall.

**[Awning Sign](https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/beaverton-or/doc-viewer.aspx#secid-1059) (secid 1059).** A sign attached to or incorporated into an awning.

**[Window Sign](https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/beaverton-or/doc-viewer.aspx#secid-503) (secid 503).** A sign permanently or temporarily attached to or placed within three (3) feet of a window to a building or structure in any manner so that the sign is used primarily as a stationary identification. Window signs include words, symbols, figures or images, paint applied to the window area, and perforated adhesive vinyl film.

**[Height of Sign](https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/beaverton-or/doc-viewer.aspx#secid-295) (secid 295).** The height of a sign measured from the finished ground level, excluding mounds, berms, etc., to the top of the sign or the highest portion of the sign structure or frame, whichever is greater.

**[Primary Building Wall](https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/beaverton-or/doc-viewer.aspx#secid-1089) (secid 1089).** For the purpose of calculating the maximum sign area, the primary building wall is the length of a single building wall positioned to face the frontage of a street. For buildings located along two or more streets, the primary building wall is the longest street-facing building façade. (A graphic in the code guides curvilinear/varied walls.)

**[Wall, Exterior](https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/beaverton-or/doc-viewer.aspx#secid-646) (secid 646).** A vertical architectural member used on the exterior of a building that extends from finished grade level to the eave of a sloped roof, or the top of a parapet. For a sloped roof without eaves, the lowest portion of the roof is the top of the exterior wall.

## Buildings / area

**[Floor Area](https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/beaverton-or/doc-viewer.aspx#secid-45) (secid 45).** The total area of a building measured from the interior of exterior walls. Excludes off-street parking (except RMA/RMB/RMC/CM-RM, where it includes garages but not carports), vehicle maneuvering/loading; areas where the finished floor is ≥4 ft below the nearest adjacent right-of-way; areas with ceiling height under 6 ft 8 in; roof area incl. roof-top parking; rooftop mechanical equipment; and roofed porches/balconies unless enclosed by walls >42 in for ≥75% of the perimeter. May be summed across multiple buildings on a site.

**[Structure](https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/beaverton-or/doc-viewer.aspx#secid-95) (secid 95).** Anything constructed, erected or built and located on or under the ground, or attached to something fixed to the ground.

**[Story](https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/beaverton-or/doc-viewer.aspx#secid-93) (secid 93).** That portion of a building between any floor and the next floor above (topmost story runs to the ceiling/roof). (See code for basement/under-floor rule.)

## Trees (these confirm the landscape vs. hazard distinction)

**[Hazardous Tree](https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/beaverton-or/doc-viewer.aspx#secid-866) (secid 866).** A tree that possesses a structural defect which poses an imminent risk if the tree (or part of it) were to fall on a target, **as determined by an arborist certified in ISA Tree Risk Assessment Qualification (TRAQ) and confirmed by the City Arborist.** Can be a dead or dying tree, dead parts of live trees, or unstable live trees. → This is the removal type that needs an arborist letter.

**[Landscape Tree](https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/beaverton-or/doc-viewer.aspx#secid-675) (secid 675).** A tree, other than a Significant Tree, Historic Tree, or tree within a Significant Natural Resource Area, that has been preserved or planted as a component of an approved landscaping plan. Community Trees that are preserved are also Landscape Trees. → Landscape removals do NOT require an arborist letter.

**[Dead Tree](https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/beaverton-or/doc-viewer.aspx#secid-661) (secid 661).** A tree that is lifeless (unseasonable lack of foliage, brittle dry branches, no growth during the growing season).

**[Protected Tree](https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/beaverton-or/doc-viewer.aspx#secid-873) (secid 873).** Outside Cooper Mountain: Significant Individual Trees, Historic Trees, trees within a Significant Natural Resource Area or Significant Grove, and Mitigation Trees. → Fee-in-lieu mitigation attaches to certain protected-tree removals, not standard landscape removals.
